Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026Version 2026-10-20.2

1. Scope and operator

This Policy explains information handling by this App and its developer. The developer identity is the information shown on the App Store product page, and contact details appear at the end. Screen images and stitching are processed locally on the device by default and are not automatically sent to the developer. Apple terms and privacy policies govern Apple handling through the App Store, screen sharing, Photos, iCloud, and other Apple services. Other recipient services you choose are governed by their own policies.

2. Data used

On-device data includes authorized screen frames, stitching segments, original and completed images; images you import or photograph, scan documents and page order, editing recipes, recognized text and exports; creation times, internal names, save progress and Photos save identifiers; crop, length, duration, watermark, language and other settings; system region; agreement version, confirmation time and region; and numerical matching diagnostics containing no recognized text. Apple handles purchases. The device verifies product identifiers, subscription periods, revocation status and the original purchase build number to confirm and restore entitlements, without reading payment-card details.

There is no account, developer-operated cloud storage, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. Location, contacts and microphone access are not requested. The camera is used only for scanning that you choose and authorize. Imports access only images selected in the system picker. ID images and recognized text may contain sensitive personal information; they are processed on-device and are not sent to a developer server. Process only necessary content for which you have a lawful basis.

Only when you voluntarily send support email does the developer receive your email address, headers, message and chosen attachments. Do not send passwords, complete ID images or unrelated sensitive information.

3. Purposes and permission

Screen frames are used to perform the scrolling match, stitching, and image creation you request. Settings, progress, and agreement records provide, restore, and configure functionality. Numerical diagnostics assess matching quality locally on the device. This local processing is necessary to provide features you actively request. Support messages are used to respond, troubleshoot, maintain security, handle rights requests, and retain records required by law. Depending on applicable law, the basis may be performance of your service request or contract, the developer’s legitimate interests, legal obligations, or consent where required. The App does not use personal information for advertising profiles or make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Sharing and transfers

The App does not automatically upload, sell, or share screenshots for targeted advertising. When you save to Photos or use system sharing, the image is provided to the Apple service, recipient, or third-party service you select. Photos and iCloud synchronization depend on your system settings.

The support mailbox currently uses email services provided by Google. After you send a message, Google and mail-routing providers may process it under their terms and privacy policies, including outside your country or region. The developer uses or discloses support information only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain security, protect lawful rights, comply with legal obligations, or respond lawfully to competent authorities, and does not sell it. Before adding remote developer processing, this Policy will be updated and required procedures completed.

5. Retention and deletion

Captures, collages, scan originals, processed results, recognized text, editing records and export caches remain on your device until you delete the relevant records, clear the corresponding data or uninstall the App. Unneeded capture segments are removed after successful image output. Capture and scan storage directories are excluded from system cloud backup. Copies exported to Photos, Files, iCloud or other services must be deleted separately. Agreement records remain until withdrawal, clearing or uninstalling, and settings can be reset. Deleting the App or local data does not cancel Apple subscriptions or delete Apple's purchase records; manage subscriptions in Apple subscription settings.

Support emails are retained only as reasonably necessary to resolve requests, maintain security, handle disputes and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized. The period depends on the request and applicable law; you may ask about it or request deletion through the privacy email.

6. Security

The App uses sandboxing, system file protection, least-privilege permissions, and local processing to reduce risk, but no device or transmission method can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Protect your device passcode and review sensitive content before sharing. Support emails are processed through email services and transmission networks, so avoid unnecessary sensitive content. The developer will meet applicable reporting and notification duties for qualifying security incidents. You must also manage risks arising from Photos, cloud synchronization, recipient services, and access after device unlock.

7. Rights and requests

You can view, edit, share, export, delete or clear local images and scan documents, and manage permissions, language, preferences and agreement choices in settings. The membership page provides Restore Purchases and access to Apple subscription management. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully completed beforehand. The developer normally cannot access data stored only on your device. For support information actually held by the developer, you may use the contact below to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent or other rights under applicable law. Identity is checked only as reasonably necessary, and requests are answered within applicable legal periods. You may also complain to your local privacy, data-protection or consumer authority.

8. Children

The App is not directed specifically to children, does not request age information, and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the App. Minors should use it with guardian guidance and only where permitted by applicable law. A guardian who believes a child submitted personal information through support email may request verification and deletion using the contact below. Processing a child’s or another person’s information still requires a lawful basis, including prior guardian authorization where required.

9. Policy updates

This Policy is available offline in the App and will also be provided on a public privacy-policy webpage. New purposes, data categories, remote services, third-party recipients, or other material changes will update the text, date, and version and provide notice or renewed consent where required by law. App Store privacy disclosures will also be updated when actual practices change. Changing the interface language alone does not change applicable law or revoke an existing confirmation.

Purchases and membership

Purchases, payments and subscriptions are handled by Apple StoreKit. The app verifies Apple-signed transactions on the device solely to confirm and restore membership, including product identifiers, subscription periods, revocation status and the build number of the originally purchased app. It does not read payment-card details or upload transactions, images or recognized text to a developer server. Apple processes purchase and account information under its terms.

Scanning and privacy

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. Photo import reads only selected images. Scanning, OCR and repair run on-device, without an account or content uploads. Originals, results and recognized text are stored locally and excluded from device backup. Delete documents or clear scan data to remove them; exported copies must be deleted separately. Repair cannot guarantee recovery of missing content. Verify IDs and contracts before use.
