The terms that apply when you use VosPad and connect to a computer.

Updated October 15, 2026

1. Service

Availability depends on the receiver, drivers and game; compatibility with every game or computer is not guaranteed.

Accepting these terms and the privacy policy does not grant system permissions. Connecting requires local network access; scanning a pairing code requires camera access; motion controls may require motion permission. Backgrounds are imported through system photo or file pickers. You may decline optional permissions, paste a pairing code manually, or browse templates and settings without accepting the agreements.

Connect only to computers you own or are authorized to use. Do not use the app to bypass device authorization, cheat, infringe others' rights, break the law, or disrupt networks or devices. Pairing codes grant access: do not share them with untrusted people or scan codes from unknown sources.

5. Fees and third-party services

Fees and terms for games, computers, networks and third-party services are determined by their providers. Any future paid features will be explained in advance and authorized through the applicable platform mechanisms.

6. Stopping use and your rights

You can withdraw acceptance at any time in Settings > Terms and privacy. Withdrawal disconnects the computer and stops input, motion controls and automatic reconnection; local templates, backgrounds and pairing credentials remain. Connecting again requires renewed acceptance. You may also delete templates, forget computers or revoke device authorization in the receiver.
