CallMe · 隐私政策

隐私政策

本政策说明“有人叫我”（CallMe）如何处理与你有关的资料。

更新日期：2026-10-11 · 版本 1.2

自定义铃声

你主动导入的提示音会在本机转换并保存，文件名和声音选择也保存在本机，不会上传。不保存倾听录音。你可在持续提醒的铃声页面删除导入声音，或通过清空全部本地数据删除。系统备份中的副本由系统设置管理。

1. 本机处理

只有在你主动开启倾听时，应用才临时处理麦克风音频。日常检测在设备本地进行。应用不保存原始音频文件、不上传环境音频或转写文本，不使用云端 AI、账号、广告、分析或跟踪 SDK。停止、撤回或进程退出后，音频和完整转写不会留存。

2. 保存什么

应用在设备上保存你输入的姓名、别名、关键词、提示文案和启用状态；只保存检测类型、匹配到的规则文本、时间、真实可用的分类置信度与检测说明。语音转写不伪造置信度，也不保留完整对话。最多保留 1000 条事件，并在成功保存新事件时删除较早记录。应用设置与同意版本、时间及系统地区提示保存在本机偏好中。

3. 权限与苹果服务

首次启动不请求麦克风。你点击开始倾听后才请求录音权限。系统通知默认启用；同意协议后首次使用会由 iOS 单独请求通知权限，你可拒绝或在设置中关闭，拒绝后仍可倾听和查看记录，但没有检测通知。苹果语音模型可能需要你主动点击下载，通过苹果系统服务安装，所需空间与下载量由系统实际模型决定，本应用不虚构固定大小。模型由苹果管理；移除应用不会保证删除系统共享模型。iOS、设备备份和你选择开启的系统诊断由苹果的规则及设置管理，本应用不接入 CloudKit 同步。

4. 通知与旁观者

通知可能在锁屏显示匹配姓名或短语，不含原始音频或完整转写。你可以关闭应用提醒，或在系统设置中隐藏通知预览。附近人说话会被短暂分析，应用不会建立旁观者身份、声纹或对话档案。检测算法存在误差，请尊重周围人的隐私。

5. 保存期限、删除与撤回

配置和事件保留到你删除、清空本地数据或卸载应用，事件另受 1000 条上限约束。同意凭据保留至撤回或清空应用数据。你可编辑与删除规则、清空历史、清空全部本地数据，或随时撤回同意；撤回立即停止采集和识别，不影响此前依法完成的处理。撤回后仍可访问并删除历史。开发者无法读取只保存在你设备内的这些数据。设备备份中的副本需按系统备份方式自行管理。

6. 支持、未成年人和更新

应用不主动收集支持资料。如你自行向发布者发送问题或资料，请避免发送周围人的声音、敏感内容或多余个人信息；由发布者按实际用途与适用法律处理。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用。涉及新的处理目的、远程服务或接收方时将更新政策，并在依法需要时重新取得确认。你可通过发布者提供的联系方式咨询适用的访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对及撤回等权利。

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CallMe handles information relating to you.

Updated: 2026-10-11 · Version 1.2

Custom ringtones

Sounds you choose to import are converted and saved on this device. File names and your sound selection also remain local and are not uploaded. Listening audio is not recorded. Delete imported sounds on the sustained reminder ringtone page or by clearing all local data. System backup copies are managed by system settings.

1. On-device processing

The app temporarily processes microphone audio only when you start listening. Detection runs locally on your device. The app does not save raw audio files or upload ambient audio or transcripts. It does not use cloud AI, accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Audio and complete transcripts are not retained after stopping, withdrawing consent or exiting the process.

2. Data stored

The app stores your names, aliases, keywords, notification text and enabled states locally. Detection records contain only the detection type, matched rule text, time, classification confidence when actually available, and detection details. Speech transcription does not fabricate confidence scores or retain complete conversations. Up to 1,000 events are kept; older events are deleted when a new event is successfully saved. Settings and consent version, time and system region hints are stored in local preferences.

3. Permissions and Apple services

Microphone access is not requested at first launch; it is requested when you tap Start listening. System notifications are enabled by default. After you accept the agreement, iOS separately requests notification permission on first use. You may decline or disable it in Settings. You can still listen and view history without permission, but detection notifications will not be shown. Apple speech models may require you to start a download through Apple system services. Storage and download sizes depend on the actual model; the app does not claim a fixed size. Apple manages the models, and uninstalling the app does not guarantee removal of shared system models. Apple’s rules and settings govern iOS, device backups and any system diagnostics you enable. The app does not use CloudKit synchronization.

4. Notifications and people nearby

Notifications may show matched names or phrases on the lock screen, but do not include raw audio or full transcripts. You can disable app alerts or hide notification previews in system settings. Nearby speech is briefly analyzed, but the app does not create identities, voiceprints or conversation profiles for people nearby. Detection can be inaccurate. Respect the privacy of people around you.

5. Retention, deletion and withdrawal

Settings and events remain until you delete them, clear local data or uninstall the app. Events are also subject to the 1,000-record limit. Consent receipts remain until consent is withdrawn or app data is cleared. You may edit or delete rules, clear history, clear all local data or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal immediately stops capture and recognition without affecting processing lawfully completed beforehand. History remains accessible and deletable after withdrawal. The developer cannot read data stored only on your device. Manage any copies in device backups through the system backup settings.

6. Support, minors and updates

The app does not automatically collect support information. If you send questions or materials to the publisher, avoid sending other people’s voices, sensitive content or unnecessary personal information. The publisher handles such information according to its actual purpose and applicable law. Minors should use the app under a guardian’s guidance. New processing purposes, remote services or recipients will require policy updates and renewed confirmation when required by law. Use the publisher’s contact details to ask about applicable rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and withdrawal.
