How to Use TuLink

Free scrolling screenshots, image stitching, document scanning and photo editing on your iPhone.

Start a scrolling screenshot

  1. Open TuLink once to review the terms and complete setup. Add TuLink's dedicated start and stop controls to Control Center.
  2. Open Control Center and tap TuLink's start control, then confirm screen capture in the Apple system prompt.
  3. During the countdown, switch to the page you want to capture and move to the starting position.
  4. Wait until capture begins before scrolling. The App does not capture your microphone.

Scroll and finish

  1. Scroll slowly in one direction and keep some visible overlap between each screen.
  2. When you reach the end, tap the finish button on the Dynamic Island, or use TuLink's stop control in Control Center. Capture ends manually.
  3. Save to Photos is on by default. With add-only Photos permission granted, results are saved automatically. If permission is declined, manage the result in TuLink.
  4. Wait for saving to finish before viewing, sharing or deleting important content.

Tips for the best result

  • Use portrait orientation and keep the device orientation unchanged during capture.
  • Scroll steadily. Avoid very fast movement, sudden jumps, or repeatedly scrolling backward.
  • Allow videos, images, and other dynamic content to finish loading before you continue.
  • Review the completed image before deleting or sharing important source content.

使用方法

  1. 首次打开图链，阅读协议并完成设置，将图链专属开始、结束控件添加到控制中心。
  2. 打开控制中心，点击图链开始控件，并按 Apple 系统提示确认屏幕采集。
  3. 倒计时期间切换到需要截图的页面，移动到开始位置；确认已开始截图后再缓慢滚动。
  4. 保持竖屏，沿同一方向平稳滚动，并让相邻画面保留足够的重叠内容。
  5. 到达页面末尾后，点击灵动岛结束按钮，或使用控制中心的图链结束控件。截图需要手动结束。
  6. 保存到相册默认开启，取得“仅添加照片”权限后自动写入相册；拒绝权限时可在图链内管理结果。
  7. 等待保存完成后再查看、分享或删除重要内容。

为了获得更好的拼接效果，请避免快速滑动、突然跳转和频繁来回滚动，并等待图片、视频等动态内容加载完成。

Free and Pro

Scrolling screenshots without custom watermarks, vertical long-image stitching, standard document scanning and image export, and basic editing, adjustments and filters for images already in the App are free.

Pro adds all collage layouts and editing tools, photo import for editing, custom capture watermarks, ID templates and layouts (mainland China only), text recognition/copy/export and PDF preview/export. Monthly, yearly and lifetime plans unlock the same Pro features. See the Terms of Use for billing details.

Open Settings → My Membership → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Previous paid-download customers receive lifetime Pro based on Apple's verified original purchase record.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime purchases do not renew.

免费版提供普通长截图（不含自定义水印）、纵向长图拼接、文档扫描及图片导出、应用内已有图片的基础编辑／调色／滤镜。专业版解锁其他工具与布局、导入图片修图、证件扫描排版（仅中国大陆）、文字识别与 PDF 功能。月度、年度及永久方案功能相同。

旧版付费下载用户按 Apple 验证的原始购买记录获赠永久专业版，请使用原购买 Apple 账户在“设置 → 我的会员 → 恢复购买”中恢复。订阅需在 Apple 系统订阅设置取消；卸载应用不会取消订阅，永久内购不自动续费。
