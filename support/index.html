How to Use TuLink

Create a clear scrolling screenshot directly on your iPhone.

Start a scrolling screenshot

  1. Open the page you want to capture and move to the starting position.
  2. Open Control Center and touch and hold the Screen Recording button.
  3. Select TuLink, then tap Start Broadcast.
  4. Wait for the countdown to finish and for capture to begin before scrolling.

Scroll and finish

  1. Scroll slowly in one direction and keep some visible overlap between each screen.
  2. When you reach the end, stop capture using the App control or stop screen sharing from Control Center.
  3. Wait until the save-complete message appears, then open Photos or TuLink to view, share, or manage the image.

Tips for the best result

  • Use portrait orientation and keep the device orientation unchanged during capture.
  • Scroll steadily. Avoid very fast movement, sudden jumps, or repeatedly scrolling backward.
  • Allow videos, images, and other dynamic content to finish loading before you continue.
  • Review the completed image before deleting or sharing important source content.

使用方法

  1. 打开需要截取的页面，并移动到希望开始截图的位置。
  2. 打开控制中心，长按“屏幕录制”，选择“图链”，然后点击“开始直播”。
  3. 等待倒计时结束并确认已经开始截图，再缓慢滚动页面。
  4. 保持竖屏，沿同一方向平稳滚动，并让相邻画面保留足够的重叠内容。
  5. 到达页面末尾后，通过 App 控件结束截图，或在控制中心停止屏幕共享。
  6. 等待出现“截图保存成功”提示，然后前往相册或 App 内查看、分享和管理图片。

为了获得更好的拼接效果，请避免快速滑动、突然跳转和频繁来回滚动，并等待图片、视频等动态内容加载完成。
